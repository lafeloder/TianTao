F01 | ADDRESSED | Carry the single-setting boundary into the headline result
Type: scope_shift | Dimension: constructs
Location: gaw-abstract-003
Original passage: Access to AI assistance increases worker productivity, as measured by issues resolved per hour, by 15% on average
Reading question: The abstract reports a compact average. It should be read as an estimate for this tool, occupation, firm, and outcome rather than a general 15% effect of generative AI. The article explicitly supplies these boundaries later.
Suggested action: Keep the setting and outcome attached whenever the 15% result is reused in a summary or map.
Already discussed: Explicitly bounded in the Introduction and Conclusion; this is an addressed scope checkpoint, not a flaw in the estimate.
Related gaw-i-012: We emphasize that these findings capture medium-run effects in a single firm.
Related gaw-viii-003: our findings apply for a particular AI tool, used in a single firm, in a single occupation, and should not be generalized across all occupations and AI systems.
